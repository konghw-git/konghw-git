## 在做什么 · What I work on

两条线，每条都是「怎么训」和「怎么测」一起做：训练信号要对准目标能力，评测分数要真的测到这个能力。<br>
<sub>Two threads, each covering both how to train and how to measure: training signals should target the capability, and scores should actually measure it.</sub>

| | 算法 · Algorithms | 评测 · Evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| **大模型 · LLMs** | SFT 与强化学习后训练、奖励设计<br><sub>SFT, RL-based post-training, reward design</sub> | 评测集效度、判分可靠性、结论可复现<br><sub>benchmark validity, judge reliability, reproducibility</sub> |
| **Agent · Agents** | 工具调用与多步决策智能体的训练<br><sub>training tool-using, multi-step agents</sub> | 轨迹级评测与环境搭建<br><sub>trajectory-level evaluation and environments</sub> |

业余写一些**本地优先的个人软件**：数据只留在自己的设备上，没有账号，没有云，没有广告。<br>
<sub>On the side: local-first personal software. Your data stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no ads.</sub>
